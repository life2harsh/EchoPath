# Exploratory Listening Study Protocol

## Objective

Compare perceived playlist quality for EchoPath, random-valid, and greedy generation while keeping seed track, target, playlist length, and constraints fixed within each comparison.

## Approval

Confirm institutional ethics or supervisor requirements before recruiting participants. Do not begin collection until the required approval or exemption is documented.

## Participants

- Target at least 12 participants for an explicitly exploratory analysis.
- Record broad listening-frequency categories rather than unnecessary personal details.
- Require participants to be at least the minimum consent age applicable to the institution.
- Exclude responses only using criteria declared before analysis.

## Materials

- Use licensed FMA excerpts.
- Normalize playback loudness consistently.
- Generate paired playlists from identical seeds and targets.
- Randomize method order and mask method names.
- Use headphones where practical.

## Procedure

1. Present the consent information.
2. Collect listening-frequency category and headphone status.
3. Explain the target genre or mood for the trial.
4. Play two anonymized playlists in randomized order.
5. Collect smoothness, coherence, target-progression, and overall-preference ratings.
6. Repeat for 8 to 12 paired trials.
7. Collect optional free-text feedback.
8. Store a random participant code without names or email addresses.

## Analysis

- Analyze ratings as paired observations.
- Report participant count, trial count, missing responses, and exclusions.
- Report medians, means, uncertainty intervals, and paired effect sizes.
- Use a paired non-parametric test when rating assumptions do not justify a parametric test.
- Correct for multiple comparisons when testing several outcomes.
- Treat findings as exploratory and avoid population-wide claims.

## Data Handling

- Store consent separately from response data if signed consent is required.
- Do not commit identifiable responses to Git.
- Publish only anonymized aggregate data and the analysis script.
- Follow the institution's retention and deletion requirements.
