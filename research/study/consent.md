# Listening Study Information and Consent

You are invited to participate in an exploratory study about automatically generated music playlists. You will listen to short playlists and rate their transitions, coherence, progression toward a stated target, and overall quality.

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time without giving a reason. The study does not require your name, email address, or account credentials. Audio playback may be stopped immediately if it causes discomfort. The expected duration is 20 to 30 minutes.

Responses will be stored under a random participant code and analyzed in aggregate. Anonymized results may be included in an academic paper or project report. Data handling and retention follow the requirements communicated by the responsible institution.

- You have read and understood this information.
- You meet the required participation age.
- You voluntarily agree to participate.
- You understand that you may stop at any time.
