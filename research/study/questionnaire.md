# Listening Study Questionnaire

## Participant Context

1. Participant code
2. Music listening frequency: less than weekly, weekly, daily, or several hours daily
3. Listening device: headphones, earphones, speakers, or other
4. Familiarity with music production or DJ sequencing: none, some, or substantial

## Paired Trial

For each anonymized pair, record the seed, target, randomized presentation order, and playlist identifiers without revealing methods.

Rate each playlist from 1 (very poor) to 7 (excellent):

1. Transitions between consecutive tracks were smooth.
2. The playlist felt coherent as a sequence.
3. The playlist progressed appropriately toward the stated target.
4. The playlist maintained useful musical variety.
5. I would choose to listen to this playlist again.

Pairwise choice:

1. Which playlist had better transitions: A, B, or no preference?
2. Which playlist was better overall: A, B, or no preference?

Optional feedback:

1. What most influenced your preference?
2. Did any transition feel especially poor or especially effective?
